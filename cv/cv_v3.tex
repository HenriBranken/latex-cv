\section*{Overview}
I am seeking a new role in Web Development, where I can build practical, user-focused applications and continue growing as a full-stack developer. To strengthen this path, I completed the HyperionDev Full Stack Web \& Software Engineer bootcamp, working with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, the MERN stack, and Java, and later served as a HyperionDev Coding Mentor (July~2024--March~2025).

My background also gives me a strong data and mathematics edge. I hold an MSc in Space Physics (\textit{cum laude}), which developed my research training, mathematical modeling ability, and scientific problem-solving skills. I subsequently spent approximately 3.5 years as a Data Scientist at Matogen Applied Insights, delivering client-facing analytics, predictive models, and business-intelligence dashboards.

I currently lecture IT modules at Eduvos (Potchefstroom Campus), which keeps me close to software-development fundamentals, curriculum refinement, and continual learning. I am drawn to full-stack development because it combines clean interfaces, robust back-end logic, and analytical thinking. While fluent in Windows environments, I also work extensively in open-source ecosystems, particularly Linux~\faLinux{\vspace*{2pt}}
